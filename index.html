current famous players

Josh Allen, Myles Garrett, Patrick Surtain II, and Bijan Robinson are four very well known current athletes in the NFL. Josh Allen is a quarterback for the Buffalo Bills, and he won the MVP (Most Valuable Player award) in 2024. The MVP is given to the best player in the league during that season and he won it in the 2024-2025 season. Myles Garrett is the best defensive player in the NFL. This june he got traded from the Cleveland Browns, to the Los Angeles Rams. Because of that trade the rams are projected to win the Super Bowl (the championship for football) this year (2026). Patrick Surtain II is a cornerback which means he defends the people that catch the ball and score touchdowns. He just led his team the Denver Broncos to the AFC Championship game which is the game before the super bowl. Unfortunatly they lost that game to the New England Patriots who played in the Super Bowl but lost to the Seattle Seahawks. He is looking to lead his team back to the playoffs this year which started on september 9th. Bijan Robinson is a running back for the Atlanta Falcons. And according to many people the most skilled athelete in all of football. He went to the University of Texas for college. And he totalled 2,298 yards in the 2025 NFL season. Which stood as the twelvth most ever by a running back. And he broke the Atlanta Falcons single season rushing yards record.

team history

The four most commonly known teams in the NFL are: The New England Patriots, The Los Angeles Rams, The Green Bay Packers, and the Kansas City Chiefs. The reason all four of these teams are globally known is because they have won a combined 21 super bowls out of a total possible 60 played. Thats more than a third of the entire super bowls! The New England Patriots had the GOAT in football Tom Brady who won 7 Super Bowls (6 with the patriots, and 1 with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at the very end of his career.), and won three NFL MVPs. The Chiefs have Patrick Mahomes who might very well be the most famous player currently playing, because of his time in Kansas City and the amount of accolades he was able to achieve. (3 Super Bowls, and 2 NFL MVPs.)
