current famous players

Josh Allen, Myles Garrett, Patrick Surtain II, and Bijan Robinson are four well very well known current athletes in the NFL. Josh Allen is a quarterback for the Buffalo Bills, and he won the MVP (Most Valuable Player award) in 2024. The MVP is given to the best player in the league during that season and he won it in the 2024-2025 season. Myles Garrett is the best defensive player in the NFL. This june he got traded from the Cleveland Browns, to the Los Angeles Rams. Because of that trade the rams are projected to win the Super Bowl (the championship for football) this year (2026). Patrick Surtain II is a cornerback which means he defends the people that catch the ball and score touchdowns. He just led his team the Denver Broncos to the AFC Championship game which is the game before the super bowl. Unfortunatly they lost that game to the New England Patriots who played in the Super Bowl but lost to the Seattle Seahawks. He is looking to lead his team back to the playoffs this year which started on september 9th. Bijan Robinson is a running back for the Atlanta Falcons.
